# Dictionary guide

This guide explains the **ON / OFF / Save** controls added by FlipSide. They appear in supported KOReader dictionary windows.

## Save a meaning

1. Open the dictionary for a word while reading or reviewing a flashcard.
2. Tap **ON** if you want to select a definition without opening another lookup.
3. **Hold, drag and release** across the words you want to keep.
4. Tap **Save**. The selected text is stored with that word and its dictionary source.

You can repeat these steps to save **several definitions**, including definitions from different dictionaries. To manage them, open **Aa → Saved meanings** in FlipSide or long-press **Save** in the dictionary. There you can edit, delete or change their order.

## ON versus OFF

| Setting | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **ON** | Select definition text; ordinary taps inside the dictionary don't open links. Tap outside to close. |
| **OFF** | KOReader's usual dictionary taps and nested lookups work again. |

Scrolling and swiping remain available. The ON/OFF preference does **not** erase saved definitions.

## Optional saved-meaning popup while reading

The **Popup** checkbox is shown only in a dictionary opened while reading a book. When enabled, a word with saved meanings can show your saved-meaning popup before the normal dictionary. Use its **Dictionary** option when you want the original dictionary result. Turn Popup off if you prefer standard lookups during reading.

## From a flashcard

- **Meaning Recall:** tap the visible word to open its dictionary.
- **Contextual Retrieval:** reveal the hidden word in the sentence first, then long-press the card to open the dictionary.
- **Sentence Card:** the sentence contains the visible word; dictionary access depends on its card interaction.

The separate **Lock this position** feature remembers a dictionary position; it is not the same as saving a meaning.
